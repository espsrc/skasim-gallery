Comparative simulations

Each case reproduces a well-known deep-field or survey configuration with matched sky inputs and imaging parameters, making it straightforward to compare sensitivity, angular resolution, and uv-coverage across facilities.

Case #1 — MIGHTEE deep field

The MIGHTEE COSMOS field observed for 4 h at L-band with MeerKAT, SKA-Mid AA* and the VLA in B and D configurations. AA* reaches 1.78 µJy/beam at 1.5″, three times deeper than MeerKAT, and every image matches the noise predicted from the radiometer equation, weighting and confusion.

Case #2 — Circular polarization in the confusion limit

A 2 h SKA-LOW-AAstar observation at 175 MHz of a 1° field filled with 60 462 T-RECS sources. Two faint, 80% circularly polarized sources lost in the confused Stokes I image are recovered blindly in Stokes V at +8.1σ and −6.3σ, with no false positives.

Exploring the chronological staged delivery of the SKA telescopes, from the initial AA0.5 verification array to the full AA4 design baseline. Each assembly is simulated on a 0.6° portion of the SKA Data Challenge 1 catalogue with sources down to 10 μJy.

About

CSIC4SKA

Preparing Spanish research groups and technologies for the Square Kilometre Array.

The project

CSIC4SKA is an espSRC-coordinated initiative with the goal of enhancing CSIC members' participation in international collaborations for SKA Key Science Projects, increasing visibility of CSIC studies within the SKA international community, and fostering scientific and technological cooperation.

skasim in CSIC4SKA

skasim is a modular SKA simulator for creating synthetic radio-interferometric observations of the SKA (Square Kilometre Array) and precursors. It bridges user-supplied sky models and simulated imaging data products, supporting feasibility studies, definition of science cases and pipeline validation for the SKA community.

FAQ

Questions about skasim

What is skasim?

skasim is a researcher-oriented simulation package for synthetic radio-interferometric observations, made for the SKA Observatory (SKAO) telescopes, SKA-Low and SKA-Mid, and for other radio interferometers. It takes a user-supplied sky model through to visibilities, dirty and CLEANed images, uv coverage and a weblog of each run. It is built on the Karabo pipeline and uses OSKAR and WSClean.

Which telescopes can skasim simulate?

Any radio interferometer: the SKAO telescopes SKA-Low and SKA-Mid at each array assembly, MeerKAT, the Karl G. Jansky Very Large Array (VLA), the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) and others. The cases in this gallery use SKA-Mid from AA0.5 to the AA4 design baseline, SKA-Low (AA*), MeerKAT, and the VLA in its B and D configurations.

What do the science cases show?

Realistic science cases simulated end to end, from a sky model built on published catalogues or galaxy models to the final images: a deep continuum field compared across four telescopes, a blind Stokes V search for circularly polarized sources, the SKA-Mid staged delivery, starburst galaxies moved to cosmological redshifts, and the neutral hydrogen (HI) kinematics of a spiral galaxy.

Where can I find the code and documentation?

The source code is on GitHub at espsrc/skasim and the documentation, with configuration examples, is at espsrc.github.io/skasim. Each case page lists the telescope, frequency, observing time and imaging settings of its runs.
